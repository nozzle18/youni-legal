# YouNI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 July 2026**

YouNI is an independent study app made by Noah Winten in Australia. This policy explains
exactly what the app collects, what it does not, and what you can do about it. It describes
the app as it actually behaves, not as a general template.

## The short version

- We collect your email address, and your study progress.
- We do **not** use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any tracking of any kind.
- We do **not** sell, rent or share your data with anyone for marketing.
- Your GPA entries and study reminders **never leave your device**.
- You can delete your account and its data from inside the app, and it is really deleted.

## What we collect, and why

**Your account.** When you create an account we store an account identifier, your email
address, and a display name if you choose to set one. The display name exists so people in
your study groups see a name instead of "Member" — if you do not set one, we do not invent
one, and we never derive it from your email address.

If you use **Sign in with Apple** and choose *Hide My Email*, we only ever receive Apple's
private relay address. We never see your real one. Apple offers us your name once, at your
first sign-in and only if you permit it; if you decline, we simply have no name for you.

**Your study progress.** Which courses you selected, the lessons you completed, your answers
and scores, the experience points and streak days that follow from them, your weekly quiz
answers, and your device's timezone. The timezone is stored because a "daily" lesson and a
"weekly" quiz have to mean something in your own local time, and because a study group's
leaderboard has to pick one week for everybody in it.

**Study groups.** If you create or join a group we store the group's name, who is in it, and
the invitations you issue. Group leaderboards show members' display names and their weekly
experience points, and nothing else.

We collect this because it is what the app is: without your progress there is no progress to
show you, and it is what lets you sign in on another device and find your work still there.

## What never leaves your device

Some things are deliberately kept local, because the app has no need to know them:

- **GPA calculator entries** — your courses, marks, weights and targets. These are stored on
  your device only and are never sent to us. They are also not backed up by us, so if you
  delete the app they are gone.
- **Study reminders** — including the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your
  device. We are not told when you study, and no reminder is ever sent from a server.
- **Downloaded course content** and your local study state.

## What we do not do

- **No analytics.** No Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no product analytics of any
  kind. We do not know how often you open the app.
- **No crash reporting.** No Crashlytics or equivalent.
- **No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.**
- **No tracking** across other apps or websites. We do not ask for tracking permission because
  we do not track.
- **No selling or sharing** your information with data brokers, advertisers or anyone else for
  their own purposes.

## Who processes your data

We use **Google Firebase** (Google Cloud) to run the service: authentication, the database
that stores your progress, file storage for course content, and the server functions that
grade your work. Google processes this data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider.
Our server functions run in Australia (`australia-southeast1`).

Apple processes your sign-in if you use Sign in with Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy.

We do not use any other third-party processor.

## How long we keep it

Your data stays while your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your
profile, your attempts, your progress totals, your quizzes and your entitlement record.

Two deliberate exceptions, so this is not misleading:

- **Study groups you created survive.** Ownership passes to another member so the group does
  not collapse for everyone else. A group nobody is left in is archived.
- **A minimal record that a deletion happened** is kept, so we can show the deletion was
  carried out. It contains no personal content.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account and data** — in the app, under Profile. This is a real deletion, not a
  flag on your record.
- **Use the app without an account** — guest mode works fully offline on the sample content,
  and nothing about your study leaves the device.
- **Turn reminders off** at any time in the app, or in iOS Settings.
- **Access or correct your data** — email us and we will help.

If you are in Australia, the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles apply. If
you are in the UK or EEA, our lawful basis for processing your account and progress is
performance of the agreement between us — we cannot run the app without it.

## Beta testing

While YouNI is in TestFlight it runs against a **staging environment**. Data created during
beta testing is disposable and may be reset or deleted at any time without notice, and it will
**not** be carried over to the released app. Please do not rely on beta progress being kept.

## Children

YouNI is intended for university-level study and is not directed at children. We do not
knowingly collect information from a child. If you believe a child has created an account,
contact us and we will remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will
say so in the app before it takes effect.
