# YouNI — Support

**Contact: writetonoahw@icloud.com**

YouNI is made by one person, so replies are usually within a couple of days rather than
minutes. Thank you for your patience.

## Before you write

**Your progress vanished, or shows as zero.** Open Profile and pull down to refresh. If you
finished lessons while offline, they sync the next time the app can reach the internet — nothing
you completed offline is lost, it is queued on the device.

**A lesson says "content hasn't been downloaded".** Go to Courses and let the course download
finish. Downloads are verified before use, so a partial download is discarded rather than shown.

**Reminders are not arriving.** Check Profile → Reminders is switched on, then check iOS
Settings → Notifications → YouNI. iOS only asks for notification permission once per install:
if it was declined, the app cannot ask again and the switch will point you to Settings.

**You want your data gone.** Profile → Delete account. It is a real deletion. Groups you created
pass to another member rather than collapsing for everyone else.

## When you write, please include

- What you expected, and what happened instead
- The screen it happened on
- Your iOS version and device
- Roughly when it happened

You do not need to include your password, and we will never ask for it.

## Beta testers

YouNI in TestFlight runs against a **staging environment**. That means:

- Your beta data is disposable and may be reset without notice
- **Beta progress will not carry over to the released app**
- Things will break — telling us is genuinely the most useful thing you can do

Bug reports from beta testers are the whole reason the beta exists. Send them.
